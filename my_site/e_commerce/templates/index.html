Our Story

A promise that started in one home kitchen.

Shurbhi began the way most good things do at home — a mother, tired of packaged oils that smelled of nothing and did little for her family's health, went back to the old wooden ghani her own mother once trusted.

What came out was slower to make, but richer in aroma, colour, and nourishment. Today, Shurbhi presses every drop of oil and mills every sack of atta the same unhurried way, so that the care a mother pours into her family's plate never has to be diluted.

— Because health should never be rushed, and family never compromised.

The Kacchi Ghani Difference

Why slow pressing means better health

"Kacchi ghani" means the oil is extracted by a wooden crusher at low speed and low temperature — nothing is heated, refined, or chemically treated away.

01

Handpicked Seeds

Only whole, sun-dried mustard, groundnut and sesame seeds sourced from trusted farms enter the ghani.

02

Slow Wooden Pressing

The wooden ghani turns gently for hours, crushing seeds at low speed so nothing is lost to heat or friction.

03

No Heat, No Chemicals

No solvents, no high-heat extraction — the natural aroma, colour and nutrients of the seed stay intact.

04

Nutrient-Rich Oil

The result is oil rich in natural antioxidants and vitamins — food that works quietly for your family's health.
